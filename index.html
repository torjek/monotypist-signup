How one hand types a full keyboard

Colemak gives each hand three rows of five keys. MonoTypist folds the right half onto the left: two characters a key.

Tap a key and it types its black legend, the letter your left hand has on a two-handed Colemak keyboard.

Hold Space with your thumb and each key types its blue legend. Space and a key pressed together type one blue letter.

Hold Enter and the keys become a number pad, with 4, 5 and 6 on the home row. The red legends show each number.

Hold Delete for the orange legends: brackets and other symbols. Tapped alone, each thumb key does what its name says.

Press the five keys from Q, A or Z on your own keyboard, with Space, Enter or Delete held. Or tap the drawing.

Keyboard controls for scrolling and pointing

Knob

Turn the knob beside ALT for Page Down one way and Page Up the other.

5-way switch

Push the cross-shaped switch to move the cursor up, down, left or right.

Touchpad

Slide a finger on the touchpad beside the keys to move the mouse pointer.

Questions

Can I remap the keyboard?

Yes. It runs the open-source ZMK firmware, so every key can be remapped.

Does the keyboard need a driver?

No. Over Bluetooth or a USB cable, the computer sees an ordinary keyboard.

Is there a right-hand keyboard?
